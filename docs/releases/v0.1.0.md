# DevType v0.1.0

Major release introducing **Smart Speech-to-Text & Dictation** powered by local, on-device intelligence (**Mistral Voxtral Realtime Mini 4B**, **Fun-ASR-Nano**, and **Apple Speech Framework**), paired with Google Gemini **Jot**-inspired thought revisions, multilingual disfluency resolution, code identifier formatting, and an authentic **Apple Liquid Glass HUD**.

## 🎙️ Smart Dictation & Voice Intelligence

- **Dual Local Voice Models**:
  - **Mistral Voxtral Realtime (Mini 4B)**: High-accuracy multimodal audio LLM for semantic context and natural punctuation.
  - **Fun-ASR-Nano (0.8B)**: Ultra-low latency edge speech recognition model optimized for Apple Silicon Neural Engine.
  - **Apple Speech Framework**: Zero-download native fallback ensuring instant offline availability.
- **Jot-Inspired Thought Revisions**:
  - Automatically resolves mid-speech self-corrections (e.g. *"Let's meet at 1:00 PM... actually 2:00 PM"* $\to$ *"Let's meet at 2:00 PM"*) in $O(N)$ linear time without regex backtracking.
- **Disfluency & Filler Stripping**:
  - Strips verbal hesitations (*"um"*, *"uh"*, *"er"*, *"like"*, *"you know"*), Korean fillers (*"음"*, *"어"*, *"그"*, *"저"*, *"저기"*), and Japanese fillers (*"えーと"*, *"あの"*, *"うーん"*).
- **Multi-Register Tone Formatting**:
  - Supports `Natural`, `Email`, `Chat`, `Code`, and `Verbatim` tones.
  - Automatic conversion for code identifiers and operators (`camelCase`, `PascalCase`, `SCREAMING_SNAKE_CASE`, `kebab-case`, `=>`, `->`, `===`, `!==`, `...`, `&&`, `||`).
- **Custom Jargon & Vocabulary Dictionary**:
  - User-configurable phonetic replacement dictionary for brand names, acronyms, and specialized terminology.

## 🛡️ Robustness & Safety Hardening

- **Watchdog-Guarded Single-Shot Transcription**: Guaranteed non-blocking completion with a 12-second watchdog guard preventing recognizer stalls.
- **Millisecond-1 Audio Journaling**: Streaming 16kHz mono 16-bit PCM capture with crash-resilient disk journaling (`active_session_*.pcm`) and isolated session UUIDs.
- **Hardware Route Interruption Recovery**: Listens to `AVAudioEngineConfigurationChange` to dynamically adapt when AirPods/headphones are connected or disconnected.
- **Push-to-Talk Debounce & Noise Rejection**: Hardened against rapid toggle bursts and pure punctuation/symbol inputs.
- **SHA-256 Checksum Verification**: Enforces cryptographic integrity before installing downloaded model weights.
