# DevType v0.0.9

Security and reliability hardening across the expansion engine, the AI pipeline,
persistence, and the app UI — plus a much deeper diagnostic report.

## Engine

- Expansion now refuses to fire when the erase-back step does not complete
  cleanly. Previously a silently failed erase injected the replacement text over
  an unerased trigger, corrupting the document.
- Backspace-after-expansion undo is hardened: real keystrokes passing through
  during delivery (including inert-key flushes) can no longer poison the undo
  record, and a refused undo always returns the swallowed backspace.
- Trigger-restore after a failed paste now consults the accessibility capability
  store instead of guessing the target app.
- Clipboard capture restores retry with ownership re-checks.

## AI safety

- Three-layer fail-closed defense against the AI prompt echoing into your
  documents: a corpus-wide leak guard (framing *and* instruction sentences),
  whole-text re-checks after chunked transforms are reassembled, and an
  injection-boundary verdict at the single choke point all AI results flow
  through. A refused payload leaves your field untouched — never data loss.
- Error and diagnostic prose is redacted of the selected text and any prompt
  surface before it reaches OSLog or the diagnostics store.

## Persistence and data safety

- Racing configuration saves join one critical section so they can never purge
  keychain secrets the winning save just wrote.
- Espanso imports and image paths are confined to the config root; absolute
  paths and `..` escapes are rejected. Malformed imported files are skipped and
  counted instead of aborting the whole batch.
- Image payloads in snippets are capped at 25 MB.
- CSV export neutralizes spreadsheet formula injection (`=`/`+`/`-`/`@` prefixes).

## App UI lifecycle

- Fixed the FillInPanel capture-list bug that pinned the panel hierarchy forever.
- Fixed a LabSession leak surfaced by the red traffic light.
- Recovery dialogs gained single-flight settling, so stacked modals can no longer
  pile up.
- Empty replacement bodies are rejected with proper English/Korean/Japanese
  messages; key monitors and listeners gained re-entrancy guards.

## Diagnostics

- The diagnostic report now carries a bounded tail of recent in-app logs
  (in-process ring buffer polling OSLogStore), including lines older than what
  `logd` still retains, with a truncation notice when clipped.

## Quality

- +38 regression tests for everything above (1202 total).
